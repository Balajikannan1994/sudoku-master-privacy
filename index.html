Sudoku Master – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Sudoku Master ("the App") is a Sudoku puzzle game developed by Balaji. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Sudoku Master application.

1. Information We Collect

Sudoku Master does not directly require users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, home address, password, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Game Data

The App may store certain game-related information locally on your device to provide its features.

This may include:

  • Saved game progress
  • Games played
  • Games completed
  • Games lost
  • Best solving time
  • Scores
  • Difficulty-related statistics
  • Game settings such as sound preferences

3. Advertising

Sudoku Master uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process certain information from the device for purposes such as displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and improving advertising services.

Depending on applicable settings, consent, and device configuration, information processed by advertising services may include advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services, including Google AdMob. These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Master is a general-audience puzzle game. The App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us using the email address below.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Game-related information stored locally on the device is subject to the security of the user's device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and statistics stored locally remain on the device until they are deleted through the App, the App's data is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.
